# Spoiler walkthrough

Interact with the labeled objects using E while aiming at them. Each floor has an optional photograph at the back left and a coin offering at the back right. Collect the photographs on the first four floors for the true ending. Notes remain available in Inventory after reading.

1. **The Basement:** Take the flashlight near the entrance. Collect the blue, red, and yellow fuses in the side rooms. Read the maintenance log. Insert **blue → red → yellow** at the panel. Enter **0413** at the security lock. At the questioning circle answer **A hole**. Use the stairway seal, visit the shop, and descend.
2. **The Forgotten Hospital:** Collect Ada, Leo, and Mia's records. Assign **Ada → Leo → Mia** to the rooms. Read the theater plate and enter **314** at its power panel. Survive the 18-second hunt by running or hiding quietly with the flashlight off. Answer the two questions from their displayed wording and the ward clues, then descend.
3. **The Hall of Mirrors:** Collect the sun, eye, and moon inscriptions. The wall order is reversed in the mirror: **moon → eye → sun**. The true path is **214**. Answer the mirror questions and descend.
4. **The Ritual Chamber:** Collect ash, bone, bell, eye, and crown. Read the litany. Place **ash → bone → bell → eye → crown**. Light the candles **2 → 4 → 1 → 3**. Survive the 28-second hunt and answer three questions. Descend.
5. **The Final Descent:** Read the keeper's journal. Name him **Elias**. Turn the wheel **ember → ash → dawn**. Open the final seal with **1906**. Survive the 35-second hunt and answer the final three questions.

At the final stairway, extinguish the candle for the **Escape ending**. With all four earlier photographs and the keeper's journal, speak his name and return the ember for the **True ending**. Chapters lets you revisit unlocked floors to recover missed photographs and return to the completed final floor.

Wrong puzzle answers lower sanity. Wrong answers to the Candleman initiate a penalty hunt; survive and return to answer again. A purchased hint token reveals the answer. Retry retains discoveries and prevents duplicate rewards. No puzzle requires an undisclosed random solution.
